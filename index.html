The briefing

 
Every heist needs someone watching the alarms. I want to be that person for real systems.

 

  

   
I am a BCA student at Teerthanker Mahaveer University, Moradabad, graduating in 2027. My goal is to work as a SOC analyst: watching logs, spotting attacks early, and helping teams respond before real damage is done.

   
I also work as a Quality Department Assistant, so I bring habits that matter in security too: checking details, following procedures, and staying calm under pressure. I learn in public, and my notes, labs and projects are on GitHub.

   
In my quality job, one missed detail can become a big problem later. Security works the same way, only the stakes are higher. I like the idea of noticing a small warning sign in a log or an email before it turns into a real attack, and that is why I chose cybersecurity.
